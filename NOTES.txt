To find an NPC's marker and model in a quest:
	Start with NPC's name: (Kinnison)
	(ffxivTool) xtx_displayName.csv client file gives [name ID] on first column
	That [Name ID] is used in (ffxivTool) actorclass.csv and (ffxivTool) quest_marker.csv
	actorclass.csv gives NPC client [Model ID] (first column)
	quest_marker.csv gives NPC client [Marker ID] (first column)
	The [Name ID] is also in xtx_quest.csv for the name of the quest giver?
		Cross-reference all these to find info for use in
	quest_template.lua and RequestQuestJournalCommand.lua and quest luas
